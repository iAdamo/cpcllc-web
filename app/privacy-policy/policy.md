Companies Center LLC operates a local services marketplace through the Companies Center mobile
applications and companiescenter.com. This Policy explains the personal information we collect, its
sources and purposes, the people and services that receive it, how long we keep it, and how you can
exercise privacy choices.

The marketplace is for adults age 18 or older. One account may be used as a Client and a Service
Provider. Payments for work arranged between Clients and Service Providers take place outside
Companies Center. Separately, Companies Center may sell its own prepaid provider tools through the
website. Optional location sharing and the Sanux artificial intelligence assistant have their own
controls, described below. A business can also use the Team feature to give the people in its own team
Team Tasks, as described in Section 13A.

## 1. Scope of This Policy

This Policy covers our iOS and Android applications, public website, account features, marketplace
records, messages, the Team feature, support, privacy and copyright requests, legal notices, and
administrative processing that supports those services. The website and applications can use the same
account and backend records. Features may differ by platform and release.

This Policy does not govern another user's independent business records, a bank or payment application
chosen to pay for a job, or an external website. A provider who receives customer information has
responsibilities for its independent use of that information. Employment and applicant records require a
separate notice where applicable; this scope distinction does not remove privacy rights that a law gives
employees, applicants, or business contacts.

Companies Center serves the United States and Nigeria. In the United States, its initial local-services
focus is Hillsborough County, Florida. Availability can vary by locality and feature. Processing by an
overseas service provider or contractor does not by itself mean that the marketplace is offered in that
country. Section 31 describes the service territory and the additional disclosures for Nigeria.

## 2. Definitions

**Companies Center, we, us, and our** mean Companies Center LLC. **Platform** means the applications,
website, and related services covered by Section 1.

A **Client** is a user seeking or managing a service. A **Service Provider** or **Provider** is a business
or independent professional publishing services, responding to requests, or using business tools. A
**Job** is a service request, either open to eligible providers or directed to one provider. A **Booking**
records a scheduled service interaction. A user may act in both marketplace roles.

A **Team Task** is a to-do a business gives the people in its own team through the Team feature described
in Section 13A. A Team Task is not a Job and is never shown in the marketplace.

**Personal information** means information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to a person or
household under the applicable law. Publicly posted information is not automatically outside this
Policy. Information is treated as deidentified only when it meets the applicable legal standard;
replacing a name with an account identifier does not, by itself, make information anonymous.

**Sensitive information** can include precise location, account credentials, financial account details,
and other categories protected by applicable law. We treat provider bank instructions as sensitive even
though identity documents, facial templates, and other identity-verification documents are not currently
collected.

A parent or guardian can contact us through Section 35 to report a child's information and request

## 11. Live Location

Live location is optional. A Client can request it from a provider profile or Booking. The provider
approves or denies the request and selects the duration where applicable. One participant's permission
does not authorize sharing another participant's device location; each person's transmission requires
their own applicable permission and agreement.

Open Profile > Live location, or the live indicator at the top of the screen, to view sessions. Use Stop
to end an active session or Cancel to cancel a pending request. A provider can turn off Allow
live-location requests in its Live location settings. Either participant can stop a session, including
one linked to a Booking. This stops sharing without itself completing or cancelling the underlying work.

Standalone sessions last 15 minutes, 1 hour, or 8 hours. Booking-linked sessions end when stopped, when
the Booking is completed or cancelled, or at a maximum of 12 hours, whichever occurs first. Expiry is
checked on a recurring schedule, with up to one minute of processing delay for the automatic duration
check. Deactivation or suspension ends active sharing; account deletion removes the sessions subject to
any lawful preservation restriction.

The app displays an active-sharing indicator. Android uses a persistent system notification and iOS
displays its location indicator while background sharing is active. Background permission is requested
for a live session; if refused, sharing is limited to operation while the application is open. Device
settings also allow permission to be revoked.

Each cached position replaces the previous one and expires 15 minutes after the most recent update. For
reconnection, the database keeps the most recent position per participant and session, including
latitude, longitude, heading, speed, accuracy, and time, refreshed at most every 30 seconds. An hourly
process removes it between 24 and 25 hours after the session ends. We do not build a route history from
these updates.

Session metadata identifies participants, linked Booking, status, chosen duration, approval and ending
times, and reason for ending. It is removed at the hourly run after 180 days from the end, allowing up to
one additional hour. Unanswered requests become eligible to close 24 hours after sending; requests for
work that never starts become eligible 24 hours after the appointment, and close at the next hourly run.
These closed requests then follow the metadata schedule.

Live coordinates are provided to the authorized session participants and systems operating the feature.
Staff have no ordinary administrative screen displaying live or last-known coordinates. Restricted
infrastructure access can still provide technical access; the absence of a staff screen is not a promise
that no authorized person could ever access stored data.

## 13A. Team Privacy

A role in a team belongs to one person in one business. A person's account remains their own, and a
person may belong to several businesses' teams.

**What the business sees.** The Owner and Managers of a team see its members' names, profile photographs
and roles, the Team Tasks of the business, and, for each person, whether they completed a Team Task,
when, their comment, and their photos. A Supervisor sees the same for the members who report to them. An
Employee sees the Team Tasks sent to them and who sent them. Before a person accepts an invite, the app
tells them what the business will see.

**What the business does not see.** Joining a team does not give the business access to the person's
messages, Jobs, Bookings, contact details, location, or any other part of their account.

**Invites.** An invite is sent by email to the address the business enters, and can be accepted only by
an account whose verified email address matches. The link expires after seven days. We store a
fingerprint of the link, not the link itself.

**Leaving.** A person may leave a team at any time, and the Owner or a Manager may remove them. Their
pending Team Tasks are withdrawn. Team Tasks they completed, with their comments and photos, remain in
the business's history.

**Notifications.** We notify a person when a Team Task is sent to them, the creator of a Team Task when
everyone has completed it, the Owner and Managers when someone joins or leaves, and a person when a
business removes them. Notifications show the Team Task title and the business name.

**Staff access.** Authorized Companies Center staff may see which businesses use the Team feature, their
members' names, email addresses, roles and membership dates, invite email addresses and statuses, and
counts, to provide support. Staff tools do not display Team Task titles, notes, completion comments, or
completion photos.

## 14. Messages, Presence, and Blocking

Direct messaging uses message content, attachments, delivery and read status, presence, typing, and
last-seen information. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized access can occur for delivery,
support, abuse or dispute investigations, security, legal compliance, and enforcement of the Terms. We do
not use private message content for advertising.

Either participant can block a conversation, preventing both from sending messages in that conversation.
This control does not by itself prevent viewing public content or using other platform features. Users
can report abuse through Customer Support. Staff have no ordinary screen for opening private chat
attachments, although authorized technical access may be possible.

Individual message deletion is not currently offered as a control in the mobile applications covered
here. Where an authorized deletion request is executed through supported account or privacy processes,
the sender's message and its attachment are erased from the active conversation for both participants.
Account deletion removes messages sent by the deleted account; messages sent by the other person remain
with that person's conversation. Separately preserved report, dispute, backup, or legal records follow
Section 26.

## 17. Sanux Artificial Intelligence Assistant

### 17.1 Purpose and Provider

Sanux is an optional chat assistant powered by Google LLC's Gemini API. It generates informational
answers about Companies Center and the questions you submit. You can use the marketplace, contact
Customer Support, and exercise privacy rights without using Sanux.

### 17.2 Separate Permission Before a Message Is Sent

Before your first message, the app and website show a notice identifying Google Gemini and explaining
that your submitted text goes to Google. No message is sent to Gemini until you select Continue. We
record the notice version and acceptance time on your account, or locally on your device if you are a
guest. An updated notice requires a new acceptance before further submissions.

Accepting the Terms or acknowledging this Policy does not by itself authorize a Sanux submission. Your
separate permission applies to the conversation you choose to send; it does not give the assistant access
to private account records or authorize advertising based on that conversation.

### 17.3 Information Sent to Google

Companies Center sends Google the instructions needed to operate Sanux, the conversation text you enter,
and relevant earlier assistant responses to generate an answer. Requests pass through Companies Center's

## 26. Retention

The schedule below identifies retention periods and deletion events by category. Where a system uses
event-based or volume-based cleanup rather than a fixed age limit, the schedule explains that
distinction. Retained account identifiers and linked records are not necessarily anonymous. Applicable
deletion rights and limited preservation exceptions apply to the information actually retained.

**Account and provider profile.** Kept while the account exists; there is no automatic inactivity purge.
A verified deletion request starts a 30-day cancellation period, followed by the next daily purge,
ordinarily by day 31. Contact fields, credentials, saved coordinates, home address, business details, and
preferences are removed. A reduced account identifier can remain with surviving records.

**Legal document and age records.** Account life, followed by retention of the document version,
decision time, platform, and age affirmation on the reduced account record. There is no automatic
age-based purge of those surviving records. They document notices and eligibility and remain subject to
applicable deletion rights.

**Verification codes and sessions.** Email codes expire after 30 minutes and reset codes after 60
minutes; used codes are removed. Refresh-token and session records expire according to the lifetime
assigned when issued, at most 90 days, and are deleted automatically at expiry. Earlier revocation applies
as described in Section 19.

**Notifications, preferences, and marketing choices.** In-app notifications: 30 days from creation.
Delivery logs: 90 days from the event. Preferences, marketing choice records, device registrations, and
push tokens: account life, with removal at account deletion and applicable device or session changes.

**Temporary live coordinates.** Cache: 15 minutes after the last update. Database resume position:
removed between 24 and 25 hours after the session ends. This does not erase a separately saved search or
Job address. See Section 11.

**Live-session metadata.** Removed at the hourly run after 180 days from session end, allowing up to one
additional hour. Closed unanswered and unstarted requests follow the same schedule. No continuing route
history is retained.

**Public posts, comments, services, and media.** Until the user deletes the item or the account. Deleting
a post or service removes its associated media, except a file still used by another item belonging to
that user. Account deletion removes the user's uploaded media, subject to the financial-document exception
below.

**Jobs and proposals.** Jobs, including unfinished drafts, remain until the Client deletes them or the
Client's account is deleted; expiration or completion alone does not remove them. Related proposals and
attachments are removed with the Job. A deleted provider's proposal text can remain on the Client's Job,
without that provider's attachments.

**Bookings.** One year after the appointment. If linked to an estimate, invoice, or dispute, kept for that
related record's period. After account deletion, a Booking can refer to the reduced account until its
retention ends.

**Team information.** Team membership records, Team Tasks, completion records, comments and photos, and
the record of changes are retained while the business's team exists, so the business keeps its history.
Team Tasks the business archives are hidden from lists and retained with the team. When a completed Team
Task is reopened, the photos added to that completion are deleted. An invite that is declined, cancelled,
or expires is deleted 90 days later. Account deletion follows Section 27.

**Reviews.** After the author's account is deleted, the rating and completion mark remain; the review
text and photos are removed. There is no separate automatic expiry for the surviving rating in the
current system.

**Direct messages.** No age-based purge. A supported sender-deletion action removes the message and
attachment from the active conversation. Account deletion removes that account's sent messages and files;
the other participant's sent messages remain. Mobile interfaces do not currently offer individual message
deletion.

**Support tickets and chat.** One year after the ticket closes. A ticket linked to a retained dispute
remains with that dispute. Account deletion removes the departing person's attachments but can leave
ticket text until the ticket retention ends. An open ticket remains pending resolution.

**Staff audit records.** One year from the logged entry, removed at the next daily retention run.

**Disputes, moderation, and fraud records.** Three years after the case closes, report is decided, or
event is resolved. Open matters remain until resolved. Records linked to a still-retained matter can
remain for that matter's period.

**Warnings, suspensions, and bans.** Three years from the warning or from the end of the restriction, as
applicable. A standing ban is retained while it stands; its post-action period starts if reinstatement
ends it. Related dispute or report retention can extend the linked record.

**Privacy and copyright filings, legal notices, and appeals.** Two years after closure, subject to a
pending linked appeal or justified legal hold. This includes privacy requests, appeals, copyright
notices and counter-notices, Notices of Dispute, and other legal notices. Records may survive account
deletion and include filing contact details, statements, evidence, how each one arrived (including the
day an email was sent or a letter postmarked), staff history, and the response. An arbitration opt-out
is kept without a time limit: it records a choice that continues to apply to any later dispute, and
deleting it would leave no proof that the person opted out.

**Estimates, invoices, and their PDFs.** Seven years from issue, including after account deletion,
subject to applicable deletion rights and specific legal holds. The retained document can contain
customer details and provider bank instructions; it is not automatically anonymous. Files are deleted
when their retention ends.

**Purchases of Companies Center tools.** Seven years from the end of the prepaid period, including after
account deletion, subject to applicable deletion rights and specific legal holds. Includes processor
reference, amount, currency, plan, period, and status; it does not include card credentials.

**Error reports and server logs.** Error reports use volume-based cleanup: when at least 500 records
exist, a daily process removes the 500 oldest. There is no fixed maximum age for the remainder. Server
console logs follow the server's configured rotation settings. Applicable deletion requests and
incident-preservation needs are considered separately.

**Referral records.** Referral codes, redemptions, qualification dates, and benefits remain after account
deletion to record rewards and prevent repeat claims. There is no automatic time-based expiry; applicable
deletion requests are assessed separately.

**Marketing suppression and newsletter.** Announcement suppression keeps a hash of the email until a
fresh opt-in, including across account deletion. The separate newsletter list retains the readable email,
source, subscription status, and unsubscribe date; unsubscribing marks it inactive rather than
automatically erasing it. It currently has no scheduled age-based purge and is not used to send
newsletters. Applicable deletion requests are handled separately.

**Sanux conversations.** Not stored in our application database; conversation text remains in interface
memory while the chat is open. Google receives submitted context and responses as described in Section 17
and applies its separate uses and retention. AI-consent version and time are account or local-device
records, not conversation content.

**Backup and deletion-ledger copies.** Database snapshots remain until they expire under the backup
rotation. Deleting an account does not immediately remove existing snapshots. A separate deletion ledger
records account identifier, deletion date, and type, including an emailed copy for restoration recovery.
These records support deletion verification and replay and remain subject to applicable minimization and
deletion requirements.

Scheduled daily deletion runs process records after the stated period ends, ordinarily within one
additional day. Live-location data follows the hourly schedule in Section 11; account deletion follows the
cancellation period and daily run in Section 27. A failed process does not extend an applicable legal
deadline.

Open support and dispute records remain pending resolution; standing restrictions remain while in effect.
A record without automatic expiration is still subject to applicable deletion and minimization
requirements. We assess retained information against its specific purpose and any exception that permits
continued retention.

The seven-year financial schedule supports accounting, document history, and disputes. It is not a
universal legal requirement to retain every field for seven years. Applicable deletion requests are
assessed for the particular record and purpose.

Backups expire under their own rotation schedule rather than the active-account deletion process. They
